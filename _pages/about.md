# About Me

Hi 👋🏻! I recently graduated from Peking University with a B.S. in Intelligence Science and Technology from the School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science (EECS).

At PKU-Agibot Lab, I worked with [Yuanfei Wang](https://yuanfei-wang.github.io), [Ruihai Wu](https://warshallrho.github.io), and Xiaojie Zhang under the supervision of [Prof. Hao Dong](https://zsdonghao.github.io).

At the [Human-Centered Robotic Lab](https://nus-hcrl.github.io/index), National University of Singapore, I worked with [Zhenhao Huang](https://zhenhaoh.github.io), [Siyuan Luo](https://www.siyuanluo.com/), and [Ziqiu Zeng](https://ziqiu-zeng.github.io/homepage/), and was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Fan Shi](https://fanshi14.github.io/me/).

I aspire to pursue interesting, insightful, and fundamental research. My current interests lie in manipulation dexterity and robustness, as well as physics-based simulation.
